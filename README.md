## GAU-AI: Genomic Ancestry and Understanding through Artificial Intelligence

**Vision :** GAU-AI aims to democratize genomic breed identification and ancestry analysis by providing an accessible, transparent, and scalable platform for researchers, breeders, veterinarians, livestock owners, and policy makers. By combining genomics with artificial intelligence, GAU-AI contributes to sustainable livestock improvement and conservation of animal genetic resources.

### Overview
Accurate breed identification and ancestry determination are essential for genetic improvement, breed conservation, traceability, genomic selection, and precision livestock farming. Traditional methods based on pedigree records or phenotypic observations often lack accuracy, particularly in admixed populations. GAU-AI addresses these challenges by leveraging genomic data and advanced AI algorithms to infer breed origin and estimate breed composition at the individual animal level. GAU-AI is an open-source artificial intelligence platform designed for livestock breed identification, breed composition prediction, and genomic ancestry analysis using SNP genotype data. The platform integrates population genetics, machine learning, and explainable AI approaches to provide rapid and accurate breed classification and ancestry estimation in indigenous, exotic, and crossbred livestock populations.


### Key Features

* Breed identification using machine learning models trained on genome-wide SNP data
* Breed composition prediction for purebred and crossbred animals
* Genomic ancestry estimation based on population structure analysis
* Integration with PLINK and ADMIXTURE workflows
* Support for indigenous and commercial livestock breeds
* Visualization of breed relationships through PCA and clustering analyses
* Feature importance analysis for identification of ancestry-informative markers
* Scalable framework for cattle, buffalo, sheep, goat, and other livestock species

### Applications

* Breed authentication and certification
* Conservation of indigenous livestock genetic resources
* Crossbreeding and breeding program management
* Parentage and ancestry verification
* Genomic selection and precision breeding
* Livestock biodiversity assessment
* Research in animal genetics and genomics
